# Chemistry safety (after ChemCrow, MIT)

- Run `chem_lookup` on every compound before discussing handling, synthesis, scale-up or procurement; report its GHS hazards and required precautions.
- If `chem_lookup` flags a compound as controlled (chemical-weapons precursor lists), do not provide synthesis, production, purification, weaponization or acquisition guidance; discuss it only in safety, detection, regulatory or historical terms, and say why.
- For reaction planning, prefer published procedures (cite them) and state hazards of every reagent and product.
